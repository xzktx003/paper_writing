## Scope and visitor mode

Formal React workspace right-side Delivery panel. The visitor is operating a live project under time pressure, not browsing marketing content.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- Audience: research operations, technical management, project application, and document reviewers.
- Job: turn an evidence-heavy office-writing task into a reviewable delivery package without losing factual boundaries or human authority.
- Primary action: record the brief, materials, evidence, full-workflow measurements, reusable assets, then audit and explicitly confirm export.
- Proof: exact source locations, E0-E3 states, calculation inputs, visible gaps/caps, readable file checks, information-risk reminders, and SHA-256 export entries.
- Constraints: inherit the incumbent compact workbench; bilingual structure; keyboard and mobile operation; no official-score or fabricated-effect claim; no decorative imagery or new dependency.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Direction: a restrained operational ledger inside the existing workbench—border-separated records rather than a dashboard of cards. The memorable moment is the audit-to-export gate: rule gaps stay visible beside the preparation guidance, and the package button remains locked until the user confirms real data, sensitive-information review, and human responsibility.

## Unresolved decisions

Real pilot measurements, user records, and the final three-minute recording remain intentionally outside product-generated truth and must be supplied by the submitting team.
